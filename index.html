Features

Everything you do. One place.

Each piece is its own thing. Turn on what you use, leave the rest.

Notes

Plain markdown with links, search, and a live preview. Files you'll still be able to read in ten years.

Tasks

A list when you need a list. A board when you need a board. Drag a card; the file updates.

Projects

Group what belongs together. Pages, files, and timelines, all in one tree you can browse like an editor.

Money

A small ledger for income, expenses, and accounts. Yours, not your bank's.

Built-in agent

An assistant that reads and edits the same files you do. Ask; it answers in your workspace.

GitHub sync

Push your workspace to a private repo whenever you want a backup or a way to share.
Who it's for

If you run a one-person operation, this is built for you.

Indie developers. Freelance writers. Side-project people. Founders of one.

You don't need a twelve-seat collaboration suite. You need a place to think, a place to keep your work, and a place to come back to it tomorrow.

Bootstrapp.ai is offline-first because your tools shouldn't go down with someone else's status page. Your data lives on your machine. The assistant is in the app, not in another tab. When you want to share, GitHub sync turns your workspace into a repo.

Free to try at app.bootstrapp.ai. Come hang out on Discord. We'd love to know what you're building.
